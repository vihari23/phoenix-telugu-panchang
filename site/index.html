Phoenix Telugu Panchangam

Daily Telugu lunar Panchang for Phoenix and Glendale, Arizona. Calculated for America/Phoenix using the Drik Ganita method. Automatically rebuilt monthly.

Apple Calendar subscriptions

  • Full daily Panchang — tithi, nakshatra, masa, paksha, special dates and festivals
  • Telugu festivals only
  • Ekadashi, Amavasya, Pournami and other observances

To keep automatic updates: on iPhone go to Calendar → Calendars → Add Calendar → Add Subscription Calendar and paste the full HTTPS URL of an ICS feed above. A downloaded import will not auto-update.

The full daily calendar includes festivals and observances, so you normally only need the first feed. Important ritual dates may vary by tradition: cross-check locally.

My family tithi dates

Create birthdays, anniversaries and yearly Telugu-tithi reminders

The personal-event planner runs in your browser and does not upload family details. It can generate a one-time ICS download for your current event list. A public automatic subscription for personal events requires separately opting in to publish those events; it is not enabled by entering a date in the planner.
